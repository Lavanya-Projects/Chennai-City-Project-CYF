CHENNAI CITY

Chennai, the capital of Tamil Nadu, is a major cultural and economic hub on the southeastern coast of India. The city, formerly known as Madras, sits along the Bay of Bengal and is renowned for its rich history, vibrant traditions, and modern development. It is one of India’s largest metropolitan areas, home to over 4.6 million residents in the city and more than 8.6 million in the metro region.

Highlights

  1. Chennai Central – iconic railway station and major travel hub

    Chennai Central is one of India’s busiest railway stations, easily recognised by its striking red‑brick Gothic design. It serves as a major travel hub, connecting Chennai to cities across the country and symbolising the city’s historic growth and movement.

  2. Marina Beach – scenic coastline and vibrant atmosphere

    Marina Beach is one of the world’s longest urban beaches, known for its golden sands and refreshing sea breeze. It attracts thousands of visitors daily who come to enjoy sunrise views, street food, and a lively coastal atmosphere.

  3. Kapaleeshwarar Temple – ancient architecture and cultural heritage

    Kapaleeshwarar Temple in Mylapore is a classic example of Dravidian architecture with its colourful gopuram and intricate carvings. It is a major spiritual and cultural centre, hosting vibrant festivals and traditional Tamil rituals throughout the year.
